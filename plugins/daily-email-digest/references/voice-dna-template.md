## ✍️ THE HUMANIZATION PASS

> Universal — pin this near the top of every `[username]-email.md`. Run all 7 in order on every draft before it ships; they make the writing read like a person, not a generator. Whole-pass goal: nobody should be able to tell a machine touched it.
>
> **Precedence:** this profile's Voice DNA and `critical_rules` always win. Where a command conflicts with a documented voice tic or a Tier-1 rule in this file, the profile wins. The pass governs *cadence* — it never overrides a hard rule (e.g. an em-dash cap).

1. **HUMAN REWRITE** — Rewrite anything that reads as *built, not spoken* — the way someone who actually went through it would say it: a bit rougher, more direct. Cut anything rehearsed or written to sound impressive. Keep the length the same.
2. **THOUGHT FLOW** — Not a grammar fix. A thinking fix. Make the ideas move the way a real mind moves: uneven in places, punchy in others, sometimes slower. Break any pattern where the writing feels too controlled or evenly paced. The rhythm of real thought — fragments, the odd dash, a sentence that opens with "And" — is allowed here *unless this profile caps it.*
3. **PATTERN DISRUPTOR** — Strip the tells that expose AI writing, one by one. Kill the three-beat fragments ("clear, honest, human"). Kill the "not just X, but Y" constructions. Kill the corporate adjectives. Make every word feel chosen in the moment.
4. **VOICE SHAPER** — Carry a clear point of view, not the tone of a neutral observer trying to please everyone. Let opinions, small contradictions, and natural shifts in tone come through. By the end, the reader should know exactly what the writer thinks.
5. **CONCRETE DETAIL** — Find the one place a real, specific detail belongs, and put it there. AI writes smooth; it can't invent your tuna sandwich on the bench. One concrete detail beats three smooth sentences.
6. **MAKE IT HIT** — The opening line has to earn the second. Same for the CTA. Any line that doesn't pull the reader forward gets cut or sharpened.
7. **CREDIBILITY TEST** *(final, adversarial)* — Read it as someone who instantly distrusts writing that sounds too clean. Go sentence by sentence and rewrite anything that feels constructed, overcorrected, or too precise. It should read like something a real person said out loud, without second-guessing every word.

*Order: 1–2 loosen the cadence · 3 strips the tells · 4 puts a spine in it · 5 adds the irreplaceable detail · 6 sharpens for impact · 7 is the skeptic's final read.*
