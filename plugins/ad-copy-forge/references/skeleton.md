# LAYER 1 — STRUCTURE

**Governance: 🔒 MUST FOLLOW — rigid gates, no exceptions**

## 1A. Section Inventory (11 possible sections)

| # | Section | Status | Appears in | Purpose |
|---|---------|--------|------------|---------|
| 1 | **Hook** | 🔒 REQUIRED | 11/11 | 2-3 short lines; pain question OR pain statement |
| 2 | **Pain Stacking** | 🔒 REQUIRED | 11/11 | 3-6 short symptom fragments (the triple-parallel pain ladder) |
| 3 | *Sound familiar?* mirror | 🟡 OPTIONAL | 5/11 | One-line recognition check |
| 4 | **Industry Reframe** | 🔒 REQUIRED | 10/11 | "Here's what nobody tells you" pivot — names the real mechanism |
| 5 | **Program Intro** | 🔒 REQUIRED | 11/11 | "Introducing [NAME]" / "Meet the [NAME]" / "[NAME] is a..." |
| 6 | **Offer Breakdown** | 🔒 REQUIRED | 11/11 | 4-6 emoji-prefixed benefit bullets |
| 7 | **Proof** | 🟢 STRONG | 9/11 | One numeric claim OR one pull-quote |
| 8 | **Qualifier** | 🟡 OPTIONAL | 7/11 | "This is for you if..." / "Designed for..." |
| 9 | **Price/Scarcity/Bonus** | 🟡 OPTIONAL | 6/11 | Low-ticket = always; high-ticket = scarcity-focused |
| 10 | **CTA** | 🔒 REQUIRED | 11/11 | Inline button OR embedded identity statement OR triple-stacked CTA menu |
| 11 | **P.S. Line** | 🟢 STRONG | 8/11 | Stakes amplifier — urgency, scarcity, or identity reframe |

## 1B. Canonical Section Order

```
1. Hook (pain question OR pain statement)
2. Pain Stacking (triple-parallel fragment ladder)
3. [Sound familiar? mirror-check — optional]
4. Industry Reframe (the mechanism pivot)
5. Program Intro (Introducing [NAME])
6. Offer Breakdown (4-6 emoji-prefixed bullets)
7. Proof (one numeric claim OR one pull-quote)
8. [Qualifier — optional]
9. [Price/Scarcity/Bonus — optional]
10. CTA (inline, identity-embedded, or triple-stacked menu)
11. [P.S. Line — strong recommendation, stakes amplifier]
```

## 1C. Section Proportions (word count %)

- Hook + Pain Stacking: **30-35%**
- Industry Reframe + Program Intro: **20-25%**
- Offer Breakdown (bullets): **25-30%**
- Proof + Qualifier: **8-12%**
- CTA + Price + P.S.: **8-12%**

Tolerance: ±5% per section.

## 1D. Transition Patterns (MUST)

**Between sections:**
- Triple-dash divider `---` separates hook block from reframe, offer block from proof
- Blank line between each sentence fragment (single-sentence paragraphs dominate)
- Tonal pivot phrases (verbatim corpus):
  - `"Here's what nobody tells you:"` / `"Here's what most people don't realize:"` / `"Here's the truth:"`
  - `"That's why..."` / `"That's where... comes in."` / `"That's what... is designed to help you do."`
  - `"But..."` (full paragraph start, never mid-sentence)
  - `"Sound familiar?"` (mirror check, full line)

## 1E. Formatting Rules (MUST)

- **Em dash `—`** is the primary connector (4-6 per ad). Not commas or hyphens.
- **Single-sentence paragraphs** dominate — each line earns the next.
- **ALL CAPS** reserved for:
  - Program names (`BAD BITCH`, `example-collective`, `LTUVG`)
  - Single-word emphasis (`WIN`, `BIG`, `NOW`)
  - Section labels (`OFFER BREAKDOWN:`, `SOCIAL PROOF:`, `BONUS:`, `P.S.`, `LIMITED TIME:`)
- **Triple-dash dividers `---`** at major pivots
- **Emoji rules:**
  - ✅ = universal benefit bullet marker
  - ✨ = feminine/spiritual brands
  - 🎮 = LTUVG/gaming brands only
  - → = process step bullets (vs. ✅ for benefits)
  - 🎁 / 💎 = bonus stack markers (stay consistent within a client)

## 1F. Transformation Rules (source page → ad)

**🔒 KEEP VERBATIM when:** line is ≤12 words AND active voice AND contains a sensory image
- Example: *"The music industry doesn't break voices. It breaks nervous systems."* (kept 1:1)
- Example: *"Your 'weird' is your edge. Your 'too much' is your magic."* (kept 1:1)

**🔒 REWRITE** everything else:
- Target compression ratios:
  - Power phrases: 1.00 (verbatim)
  - Explanatory prose: 0.30-0.50
  - Feature lists: 0.10-0.20
  - Founder origin stories: 0.30-0.50
- First to cut: adverbs, "step-by-step," "really," "actually," throat-clearing phrases

**🔒 ADD** (not in source):
- Numeric proof lines ("€0 to €15k/mo in 3 months")
- Scarcity phrases ("Only 4 spots per tier")
- P.S. lines (always ad-only)
- Single-line bullet benefit statements

**🔒 REMOVE**:
- Long frameworks/stage lists
- Full origin stories
- Testimonial grids (reduce to 1-2 quotes max, or 1 abstracted proof line)
- FAQ blocks
- Guarantee language
- Price stack tables (reduce to single payment plan line)

# LAYER 2 — WORDS

**Governance: ⚖️ MIX — signature moves rigid, content creative**

## 2A. Sentence Opener Library

### Pain openers (🔒 MUST use at least one as scroll-stopper)
- `"You've [verb]..."` — 11/11 ads use this form. The #1 opener.
  - *"You've been told you don't have 'natural talent.'"* (Lux #1)
  - *"You've built the business. Hit the revenue targets."* (example-collective S2#1)
  - *"You've studied Human Design."* (SU #1)
  - *"You've read the books. Watched the videos. Followed the gurus."* (LTUVG #2)
- `"Your body..."` — 4 appearances
- `"Tight [body part]..."` — 3 appearances (somatic symptom fragment)
- `"Successful entrepreneur..."` — identity-fragment opener

### Pivot openers (🔒 MUST use to hinge into reframe)
- `"Here's what [nobody tells you / most people don't realize]..."` — 4x
- `"Here's the truth:"` — 2x
- `"That's why..."` / `"That's where..."` / `"That's what..."` — 5x
- `"But..."` as paragraph start — 6+x

### Program intro openers (🔒 MUST use one)
- `"Introducing [NAME]..."` — 3x
- `"Meet the [NAME]..."` — 1x
- `"[Product name] is/uses/helps..."` — 4x

### CTA openers (🔒 MUST use one)
- `"This is for..."` / `"This isn't for everyone..."` — 5x
- `"If you're ready..."` — 3x
- `"Ready to..."` / `"Ready for..."` — 2x

### P.S. openers (🟢 STRONG when using P.S.)
- `"P.S."` — 5x opener

## 2B. Vocabulary Buckets (🎨 CREATIVE — flex with client ETHOS)

Use these as inspiration/reference, not a fixed word list. Match client's authentic vocabulary.

### Body/somatic (high-frequency in corpus)
nervous system (13x) · body (22x) · shoulders · breathing/breath · shallow · tight · adrenaline · cortisol · fight-or-flight · bracing · sensation · somatic · regulate · embodiment/embodied (12x)

### Identity/naming-the-problem
broken (signature: "not broken") · performing (11x) · surviving · successful on paper · high-performer · the machine · hero (in someone else's game)

### Scarcity/urgency/proof
Only 4 spots · First 10 only · Limited time · expires · 80% off · thousands · Over 3,000 people · €0 to €15k/mo · best-selling

### Reframe pivots (mechanism words)
missing piece · truth · real thing underneath · deeper fear · what changes · nobody tells you · most people don't realize

### Transformation verbs
shift · release · regulate · reconnect · anchor · reclaim · unlock · translate · embody · move from · calm · access

### Offer containers
experience · immersion · protocol · framework · incubator · program (often negated: "not another program") · session · container

### Client-specific metaphor families
- **Gaming (LTUVG):** power-up · level · checkpoint · skill tree · instruction manual · Spirit Avatar · playing to win
- **Body/leadership (example-collective):** ceiling · machine · running on X · grounded clarity · calm dominance · fight-or-flight · embodied leadership
- **Stage/voice (example-artist):** stage · voice · seen · shine · star power · touring · regulate under pressure
- **Chart/design (Sacral Uproar):** chart · design · holding · translate · bracing · information vs embodiment

## 2C. Pronoun Density Rules (🔒 MUST)

- **"you/your" per 100 words:** 12-16 range
- **"I/my":** Use sparingly. Only for founder-voice ads (example-artist style). Program-centric ads (example-collective/LTUVG/Sacral style) use zero "I."
- **"we/us":** Rare. Use only for the voice of the program delivering the transformation ("we translate your chart").
- **90%+ pronoun weight** must fall on second person.

## 2D. Sentence Length Rhythm (🔒 MUST)

- Maximum single sentence: **~24 words**
- Never stack two long sentences back-to-back
- **3-to-5-word punches** must appear at mechanism reveals and emotional inflection points
- Pattern: medium-heavy explanatory prose + 3-5 word fragment punches at pivots
- Fragment clusters (2-4 fragments of 2-5 words each) = load-bearing unit, not single fragments

## 2E. Signature Power Phrases (🔒 MUST use ≥1 per ad)

At least ONE of these must appear in every ad:

1. **Triple-parallel pain ladder** (9 corpus instances — the #1 fingerprint)
   - *"Tight shoulders. Shallow breathing. Bad sleep."*
   - *"You've read the books. Watched the videos. Followed the gurus."*
   - *"Still performing. Still bracing. Still trying..."*

2. **Negation-reveal** (5 instances)
   - *"Not because [expected reason]. Because [real reason]."*
   - *"Not because they suddenly 'found talent.' Because they healed the part of them that was afraid to shine."*

3. **Contrast reframe** (5 instances)
   - *"This isn't [X]. It's [Y]."*
   - *"This isn't another business program. It's a complete nervous system recalibration."*

4. **Transformation couplet** (9 instances — often used as headline)
   - *"From [bad state] to [good state]."*
   - *"From Tone Deaf to Touring Pro."* · *"From Survival Mode to Leadership."*

5. **Stop/Start chiasmus** (4 instances)
   - *"Stop [failing strategy]. Start [new identity]."*
   - *"Stop performing. Start being."* · *"Stop surviving and start playing to win."*

## 2F. Rhetorical Devices (🎨 CREATIVE — use as toolkit)

- **Antithesis** — A vs B contrasts ("doesn't break voices. It breaks nervous systems.")
- **Anaphora** — repeated sentence openers ("Still performing. Still bracing. Still trying...")
- **Tricolon** — rule-of-three parallel clauses
- **Chiasmus** — ABBA structure ("Stop performing. Start being.")
- **Metonymy** — body-part for emotion ("your shoulders paying the price")
- **Rhetorical questions** — ONLY at opening hook or recognition-check. Never mid-argument.
- **Paradox** — "Successful on paper. Emotionally disconnected in reality."

## 2G. Verb-First Bullet Architecture (🔒 MUST)

**Rule:**
- Internal outcome bullets → verb-first (Release · Regulate · Move from · Reconnect · Embody · Build · Develop · Master · Anchor · Experience · Access · Translate · Discover · Lead)
- Tangible deliverable bullets → noun-first (A digital book · Interactive app · Live ceremonies · Cyprus retreat · Coaching support)

**Verb families:**
1. **Release-verbs** — remove what's blocking (Release · Quiet · Calm · Clear)
2. **Build-verbs** — construct what's missing (Build · Develop · Master · Anchor)
3. **Access-verbs** — activate what's dormant (Reconnect · Reactivate · Unlock · Experience · Access · Translate · Discover)

## 2H. Scroll-Stopper Rules (🔒 MUST)

- **73%+ statements, not questions** (corpus: 8 statements / 3 questions out of 11)
- **First 5 words:** include "you/your" 80%+ of the time
- **First 5 words syllable count:** 5-8 range (monosyllabic-heavy)
- **Emotional valence:** tension OR recognition OR curiosity-gap. Never aspiration. Never promise.
- **Never open with:** "Discover how...", "Imagine if...", "Have you ever wondered..."

## 2I. Word-Level Compression Laws (🔒 MUST)

1. Power phrases survive at 1.00 (verbatim preservation when source has a winner)
2. Explanatory prose compresses to 0.30-0.50
3. Feature lists compress to 0.10-0.20
4. Founder origin stories compress to 0.30-0.50 (keep fragment triple + "now" pivot, cut interior narration)
5. First to cut: adverbs · "step-by-step" · "really/actually" (when non-load-bearing) · throat-clearing

# LAYER 3 — ENERGY

**Governance: 🧭 GUIDED — principles, applied with judgment**

## 3A. Entry→Exit Belief Shift (🔒 MUST)

Every ad performs a single belief-transfer:

```
[Entry belief: blamed SURFACE object]  →  [Bridge: pivot line]  →  [Exit belief: unseen MECHANISM]
```

**Examples from corpus:**
- Lux #1: "I don't have talent" → *"The music industry doesn't break voices. It breaks nervous systems."* → "My nervous system is what's breaking"
- example-collective S1#2: "I need better strategy" → *"It's your nervous system."* → "My nervous system is the bottleneck"
- LTUVG #1: "I'm broken" → *"You're not broken. You're just playing without a strategy."* → "I'm playing without a strategy"
- Sacral #2: "Information = transformation" → *"Information doesn't automatically become embodiment."* → "I need embodiment, not more info"

**Mechanism:** Blame redirected from surface (talent, strategy, discipline, chart-literacy) to unseen mechanism (nervous system, inner child, instruction manual, embodiment).

## 3B. Emotional Arc — The 4-Beat Pulse (🔒 MUST)

```
Hook-pain → Pain-stack → PIVOT LINE (engine) → Proof → Offer
```

The **pivot line is the engine.** Every other beat exists to make the pivot line land. If the pivot is soft, the ad fails regardless of bullet quality.

Full reader-state progression:
```
Scrolling (cold)
  → Recognized / seen
    → Tension (somatic or identity)
      → PIVOT / Release (mechanism revealed)
        → Hope / Credibility
          → Desire (bullets)
            → Commitment (CTA)
```

## 3C. Somatic Triggers (🔒 MUST — deploy in order)

1. **Contraction** — staccato fragments force shallow reading breath (tight shoulders, shallow breathing, bad sleep)
2. **Recognition hit** — paradox that names reader exactly (crushing it externally BUT body screaming)
3. **Expansion** — one-line exhale (Now I tour the world)
4. **Release** — the mechanism reveal, short isolated sentence (It's your nervous system.)
5. **Activation** — imperative verbs in parallel at CTA (Stop performing. Start being.)

## 3D. Pacing Dynamics (🧭 GUIDE)

- **Deceleration moments** — force a pause at mechanism reveals (fragments, ellipses, one-word sentences)
- **Acceleration moments** — build momentum via bullet cascade approaching CTA
- Pattern: fragments at top for tension · paragraphs in middle for mechanism · bullets at bottom for momentum

## 3E. Shadow/Light Balance (🧭 GUIDE)

- **Overall ratio:** ~60% shadow / 40% light
- **Pivot point:** 50-60% into ad
  - Before pivot = shadow (name the pain)
  - After pivot = light (name the transformation)
- **Client calibration:**
  - Sacral Uproar: ~70/30 shadow-heavy
  - example-collective: ~65/35 shadow-heavy
  - LTUVG: ~55/45 balanced (gaming reframe carries light)
  - example-artist: widest swing (deepest shadow → brightest light)

## 3F. Trust-Building Mechanisms (🔒 MUST)

**Techniques ranked by frequency (all 11/11 ads use #1-#3):**

1. **Specificity in symptoms** — "Tight neck. Shallow breathing." proves "I've seen you." 🔒 MUST
2. **Paradox acknowledgment** — "successful on paper, disconnected in reality" 🟢 STRONG
3. **Naming what nobody names** — "a thought you don't say out loud: Am I broken?" 🟢 STRONG
4. **Quoted authority at tension peak** — rare; use sparingly (LA Producer quote in example-artist)
5. **Insider vocabulary** — nervous system regulation, shadow patterns, embodiment (shibboleths)
6. **Quantified proof beats** — numbers placed at tension peaks

**🔒 NEVER USE:** overt credentialing · "As seen in Forbes" · "25 years of experience" · title-flexing · résumé language

## 3G. Shame/Honor Axis (🔒 MUST be HONOR-first)

- **95% honor · 0% shame**
- Open by honoring what the reader has built
- Name the paradox of that achievement (*"You've built the business. But your body is screaming."*)
- Shame-adjacent moves are momentary and always redirected to mechanism (not reader)
- **NEVER** blame the reader for being stuck

## 3H. Permission-to-Stop Moment (🔒 MUST include)

Every ad grants permission to put down an exhausting identity:

| Client | Common permission |
|--------|-------------------|
| example-artist | Stop self-blaming for lack of talent · Stop searching for talent already in you |
| LTUVG | Stop self-diagnosing as "broken" · Stop consuming without applying |
| example-collective | Stop performing · Stop hustling from your head · Stop overriding your body |
| Sacral Uproar | Stop intellectualizing identity · Stop bracing |

**Rule:** The permission-to-stop is the *hidden offer*. More load-bearing than the actual offer, because by the time the reader arrives at the bullets, the ad has already given them something for free: a reason to exhale.

## 3I. Tension-Relief Curve (🧭 GUIDE)

- **Peak tension:** 30-40% into ad (earlier = too abrupt, later = reader bounces)
- **First relief:** 45-55% into ad (earlier = sales-y/premature, later = reader disengaged)
- **Sustained moderate tension** through bullets (each bullet still names a problem-to-solve)
- **Final gentle release** at CTA
- Pattern: one sharp peak · one sharp release · sustained tension through bullets · gentle release at CTA

## 3J. Exit Velocity (🔒 MUST for transformation offers)

**Target exit state: IDENTITY CONFIRMATION** (corpus: 8/11 ads)

The reader clicks not because they're afraid to miss out, but because they've been shown a version of themselves they want to inhabit. **The CTA is a doorway to an identity, not a discount.**

**Secondary exit states (use as backbeat, not primary):**
- Hope (4/11)
- Urgency (3/11 — scarcity lines at bottom)
- Curiosity (2/11)

**🔒 MUST for transformation/healing/coaching offers** — identity exit
**🟡 MAY flex** to urgency-first for commodity or deadline-bound offers

# APPENDIX B — Counsel Debate Resolutions

### DEBATE: Industry Reframe — mandatory or optional?
**Resolution:** Default-on. Omit only if ad is under 100 words. Load-bearing for belief-shift longer-form copy.

### DEBATE: P.S. Line — scarcity-only or multi-purpose?
**Resolution:** Stakes amplifier. Scarcity is ONE form; identity reframe, warning, and deadline all qualify. Bencivenga's reading wins — P.S. carries the second most important idea after the headline.

### DEBATE: Verbatim quotes from source — copy or rewrite?
**Resolution:** Keep verbatim when source line is ≤12 words AND active voice AND sensory image. Rewrite everything else.

### DEBATE: 3-word vs 5-word punch — which is stronger?
**Resolution:** Unit is the fragment CLUSTER (2-4 fragments of 2-5 words each, totaling 8-15 words), not the single fragment. Descending syllable count is the punch rhythm.

### DEBATE: Pronoun density vs verb-first bullets — which is #1 driver?
**Resolution:** Pronoun density = scroll-stopper driver (first 15 seconds). Verb-first bullets = conversion driver (final 15 seconds). Both required, sequenced correctly.

### DEBATE: Shadow or light — stronger lever for Meta scroll?
**Resolution:** Open with honor, pivot to shadow within 2 lines. Honor-surface, shadow-buried. Shadow is faster for cold recognition; honor outperforms for warmer traffic.

### DEBATE: Full relief or just permission?
**Resolution:** Ad delivers permission (to stop the failing strategy) + glimpse of relief (the destination state). Full relief is held inside the offer. Mid-zone between pain and arrival = where clicks live.

### DEBATE: CTA exit state — urgency, identity, or curiosity?
**Resolution:** Match CTA exit-state to offer's core promise. For transformation/healing offers: IDENTITY CONFIRMATION. For commodity/deadline: urgency. Forced urgency on transformation offers reads as manipulative and suppresses conversion.
