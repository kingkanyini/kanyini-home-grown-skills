## ENERGY MAPPING

- **Entry belief:** "My breakdowns mean I'm broken"
- **Pivot line:** *"You're not broken. You're just playing without a strategy."*
- **Exit belief:** "My breakdowns are unplayed power-ups"
- **Honor move:** Reframes the reader's struggle as strategic, not defective
- **Permission to stop:** Stop self-diagnosing as broken
- **Somatic trigger:** "fear of failing is louder than the dream of winning" (tension → recognition)
- **Shadow/Light ratio:** ~55/45 (most balanced in corpus — gaming reframe carries inherent light)
- **Exit velocity:** Identity confirmation + gentle urgency ("this is for you")

## WHY SELECTED AS STYLE SHOT / GOLD STANDARD

1. **Gaming metaphor integration:** Full LTUVG ETHOS preserved (power-ups, level, checkpoints, skill tree, Spirit Avatar, DLC) — demonstrates client-specific metaphor family discipline.
2. **Noun-first bullets exception:** Perfect case study of the noun-first bullet rule (tangible deliverables), contrasting with verb-first style shot 01.
3. **Low-ticket architecture:** Shows how price anchor + bonus stack integrate without breaking the 150-250 word target.
4. **Balanced emotional arc:** Gaming reframe makes this the most light-carrying ad — useful for calibrating non-somatic brand work.
5. **Triple-parallel pain ladder:** Classic "Bad days... Old mistakes... Decisions..." ladder — textbook signature move.
