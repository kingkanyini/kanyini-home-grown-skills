**2. A Yes To a No, Transforms a No to a Yes, Faster Than a No to a No.**

I spoke about this earlier last week, but it's so good, I had to bring it back.

Honestly, I got so many questions on this, that I felt it important to give another example.

Let's say you want to learn karate.

And there is a guy you meet who is a karate master.

You ask him to train you.

He says, yes, but first, you must wash his car.

You think he isn't serious, so you refuse.

At the same time, this other kid says yes, and starts washing his cars, even if he doesn't know what this has to do with learning karate.

You come back a month later, that kid is still washing the cars, muttering, "wax on, wax off."

You laugh at him, meanwhile, you haven't started your training at all.

Two months later, this crazy kid is now washing the guys fences! HA!

"Sucker you think," as you stroll by.

A year later, you're watching the TV, this same kid, is now fighting in the championship, and defeats Cobra Kai!

"Son of a..."

The Universe works in this way.

Oftentimes when we ask for something, it presented with a pathway we dislike.

Oftentimes we resist this pathway, and think that by choosing our own pathway we'll get there faster.

The next time, say Yes to the thing that makes us uncomfortable, and I promise, it will lead you to you desired destination faster.
