# THE Subconscious Seduction AD SYSTEM (provenance note)

This file records where the skill came from. It is not loaded during a run.
The original two-prompt pack is kept in the author's private archive and is not shipped with this plugin.

## Where it started

The skill grew out of a two-step prompt pack built on Vince Reed's video ad framework:

1. Paste the offer, answer 7 intake questions, extract 14 psychological triggers.
2. Paste the triggers back in and write 14 video ads, one per trigger.

The skill keeps that spine and replaces the copy-paste steps with intake, counsel review,
a Foreman, parallel writers and a Boss review.

## Vince's original structure → this skill's 7 parts

| Original pack | This skill (main file, "Vince Reed's 7-Part Ad Structure") |
|---|---|
| Contrarian overlay hook | 1 Overlay Hook + 2 Spoken Hook (split into two units, R3 and R4) |
| Opening Realization ("I thought X was the problem…") | 3 Opening Realization |
| The REAL Problem Reveal | 4 The REAL Problem Reveal |
| The Simple Shift | 5 The Simple Shift (its own beat, R2) |
| Proof Layer + Social Proof | 6 Proof + Social Proof (R8, R9) |
| Soft CTA | 7 Soft CTA (set per funnel in Phase 1B, R10) |

## The 5 trigger-output fields

Every trigger in Phase 2 still carries the pack's five fields:
Trigger Name, Emotional Angle, Core Belief Shift (FROM / TO), Why This Works for This Audience,
and an Ad Angle (one sentence, or an overlay + spoken pair).

## What changed

- The pack's fixed VSL CTA and fixed voice are gone. The CTA comes from the funnel type, and the
  voice comes from the loaded voice profile.
- The pack's single contrarian overlay style became five assigned overlay formulas (HW, SS, WI, DR, QO)
  plus seven spoken hook codes (Q, S, C, M, SO, D, P).
- The quality benchmark is `gold-standard-ads.md` and `gold-standard-triggers.md`.

## House Rules govern

Where anything in the archived pack differs from the skill, the House Rules (R1 to R17) in
`commands/ss-ad-generator.md` govern.
