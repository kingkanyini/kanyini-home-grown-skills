## ENERGY MAPPING

- **Entry belief:** "I don't have natural talent"
- **Pivot line:** *"The music industry doesn't break voices. It breaks nervous systems."*
- **Exit belief:** "My nervous system is what's breaking, not my voice"
- **Honor move:** "You've felt the calling your whole life" (implicit honor of artistry)
- **Permission to stop:** Stop blaming talent
- **Somatic trigger:** "freeze when it's time to perform" · "shut down when people watch"
- **Founder-I usage:** 4 "I" hits clustered in one transformation proof paragraph
- **Exit velocity:** Identity confirmation ("your 'weird' is your edge")

## WHY SELECTED AS STYLE SHOT / GOLD STANDARD

1. **Verbatim preservation:** The "music industry doesn't break voices" line is kept 1:1 — demonstrates the source-preservation rule.
2. **Founder-voice template:** One of only 2 ads in corpus using "I" — demonstrates the founder-voice pattern for skills needing it.
3. **Transformation couplet headline:** "From Tone Deaf to Touring Pro" is the archetype of the signature couplet form.
4. **Section clarity:** Clean, canonical section sequence — ideal template for Phase 2 translation.
