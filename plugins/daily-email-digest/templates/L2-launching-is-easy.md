# Sequence A: High-Ticket Launch (9 Emails)

## Campaign Overview

This sequence was used to launch "High Ticket Words" - a masterclass on selling high-ticket programs using written words instead of sales calls. The sequence follows a validation-first approach: test demand before building, then build anticipation with gamified bonuses, launch with urgency, and close with belief-shifting content and deadline pressure.

**Email flow:**
1. **Product Validation** - Test if there's demand before creating the offer
2. **I'm Gonna Do It** - Announce you're moving forward + gamify with bonuses
3. **1 Hour Heads Up** - Build anticipation right before launch
4. **Main Launch / Link** - Open enrollment with the registration link
5. **PostScript Selling** - Soft sell in a P.S. attached to a value-add email
6. **Desire + Closing Enrollment** - Tap into desires + announce enrollment deadline
7. **Contrary Statement** - Challenge a limiting belief to differentiate your offer
8. **Silly Rule** - Call out an industry "rule" your offer breaks
9. **Last Call** - Final push with full feature list and hard deadline

## Email 1: Product Validation

### Strategy Notes

*This is the first email that opens the conversation. The point of this email is to be short, direct and get people to raise their hands. If I get enough responses, then I'll do the training. If there aren't enough responses, then I don't spend time making a product that won't sell. It's that simple. This is the key validation email. You can also use this as a post/story on social media.*

### Example by Joshua Sprague (2HB)

**Subject:** Do you sell anything high ticket?

Hey,

Do you sell anything high ticket?

Like a coaching program, mastermind, or live events?

Or do you want to?

And do you want to sell those without sales calls (or at least get rid of 95% of the calls)?

In the last month I did over $147k in high ticket sales just from some simple emails and an ugly Google document.

I had two 15 minute calls just to answer some questions.

I thought maybe you'd like to see how I did it.

I'm thinking of putting together a paid masterclass to show you exactly what I did.

Even though it's worth thousands of dollars to learn what I did, the training wouldn't cost anywhere near that.

I'd probably charge somewhere in the range of $200 to $400.

If that's something you'd want to learn and pay for, then just reply to this saying "HT Training."

If I get enough responses, I'll put it together.

If not, then I'll just keep it to myself.

Let me know,
Joshua "emails and G Docs" Sprague

### Practice Questions

1. What is the ONE core result your offer delivers that you could ask about in one sentence? (e.g., "Do you want to heal your gut without restrictive diets?")
2. What specific wins or results have you (or your clients) achieved recently that would make someone's ears perk up?
3. What is the main challenge or objection your audience faces that your offer specifically solves differently?
4. What reply keyword would feel natural and exciting for your audience to type? (Keep it short and memorable.)
5. What price range feels like a no-brainer relative to the value you deliver?

## Email 6: Desire + Closing Enrollment

### Strategy Notes

*This email is about clarifying what people do and don't want, and tapping into their desires. With this email, I'm tapping into the desire of those who want to know how to sell their high ticket offers without sales calls. Also, I included a deadline for enrollment to build urgency. Think about your own offer and how you can tap into the desire that your audience wants and how your new offer helps them accomplish that.*

### Example by Joshua Sprague (2HB)

**Subject:** "What do you want?"

Most people don't do the things that they want because they don't see how it's possible.

Yet HOW is the least important.

There are many HOWS.

But it must first be preceded by the WHAT.

As in...

***What do you want?***

When most people answer this, they describe it with an absence of something.

For example, I don't want to work at this job.

That's fine.

But your desires become more potent when you are moving towards something specific rather than away from something.

Instead of "I don't want to work this job" try something specific like this:

"I want an online business that allows me to get paid to write and makes $20,000 a month or more."

See how clear that is?

I did the same thing recently.

I had a new high ticket offer I wanted to sell.

I don't like doing sales calls. I didn't want to do them with that offer.

I also didn't want to create a big webinar presentation to sell it.

I wanted it to be simple.

What I wanted was to sell out the offer, make at least 6 figures from it, and use my written words to do it.

The WHAT came before the HOW revealed itself.

It also highlights the unique way that I sell high ticket offers. Think of something that you see as common in your niche or industry and talk about why it's silly. Then position your product as the smart alternative.*

Or do a "strategy session"...

Or a "discovery call"...

Or any of these other beliefs that have been proliferated by the coaching industry.

But it's not true.

Calls are ONE way to sell high ticket programs...

But not the ONLY way.

Personally, I prefer things that allow more leverage.

I prefer things that allow me to sell my stuff on a timeframe that also lets me live the rest of my life.

Because there is literally nothing more annoying than having a call scheduled and someone ghosts on you.

And they usually ghost on you because they know you're not there to do a "strategy session."

They know you're there to sell them something.

And no one likes to be sold to.

But people LOVE to buy things that they want and can help them.

That's why I like using my written words to sell high ticket things.

It lets me respect my time and my customers' time.

You read my words. You read the written assets that I've created to let you know what's available. You tell me you're in and we get going.

# Sequence B: Low-Ticket Launch (12 Emails)

## Campaign Overview

This sequence was used to launch "Money Making Meditations" - a low-ticket program ($50-$500 price point) on creating profitable audio meditations. Unlike the high-ticket sequence, this one uses a longer runway with more value-add emails, social proof through clarification, and urgency through price increases rather than enrollment closing. It also includes an early-bird segment for people who marked themselves interested.

**Email flow:**
1. **Offer Validation** - Test demand with a simple reply-to-buy question
2. **Value Add + Postscript Priming** - Give value, then prime for the upcoming offer in the P.S.
3. **Building Desire** - Announce you're moving forward + gamify with bonuses + interest link
4. **Pre-Launch Email** - Build desire and prime people to buy the next day
5. **Early Bird Launch** - Special early access for people who marked interested (30 min head start)
6. **Main Launch Email** - Open enrollment to the full list
7. **What Not How Sales Email** - Teach high-level "what" and position the program
8. **Clarification Sales Email** - Correct a misunderstanding to add clarity and sell
9. **What Not How + Building Urgency** - Another "what not how" angle with urgency
10. **Value Add + Soft Sell** - Inspire them with content, soft sell in the P.S.
11. **Closing Day** - Personal story + price increase deadline (send 2-4 emails this day)
12. **Final Email** - Direct, no-frills last call before price goes up
