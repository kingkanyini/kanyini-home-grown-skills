## Identity & Credibility (manual)

Aleyda Solís is the founder of **Orainti** (founded 2014), a boutique international-SEO consultancy serving global e-commerce, marketplace, and SaaS clients. She now brands herself, in her own current title, as an **"International SEO & AI Search Optimization Consultant"** — the AI-search line is hers, not retrofitted ([aleydasolis.com](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/), [orainti.com](https://www.orainti.com/)). She came into SEO around 2007 as a *front-end web developer* who wanted to drive traffic to the sites she was building — which is why her frameworks are so systems-oriented and operational. She publishes through the **#SEOFOMO** newsletter (35,000+ subscribers, one of the most-read SEO newsletters in the world), the free **LearningSEO.io** roadmap, the free **LearningAISearch.com** roadmap (actively maintained — "Version 4, March 2026"), the **Crawling Mondays** video/podcast series, and bylines at Search Engine Land, SEJ, Moz, HubSpot, and Think with Google. She has spoken at **200+ conferences across 30 countries**, bilingual in English and Spanish (BrightonSEO, SMX, MozCon, INBOUND, Pubcon, SearchLove). Recognition includes **European Search Personality of the Year (2018)** and USA Today / List Wire's *10 Most Influential SEO Experts of 2022*. Webflow named her one of its **"AEO Voices to Watch for 2026"** alongside Rand Fishkin and Lily Ray ([seroundtable.com](https://www.seroundtable.com/photos/aleyda-solis-european-search-personality-award-25756.html)). - **Vocabulary she uses:** topical authority, semantic relevance, citation-worthiness, query fan-out, share-of-voice, crawlability, indexability, chunking, atomic units, multi-modal, agentic commerce, findability.

She refuses both the "AI changes nothing" and "AI changes everything" camps — she'll show you the data that says it's 1–2% of traffic today *and* the framework for when that changes.

## How They Think (curated, evidence-gated)

### Mental Models
1. **Findability over rankings.** The job is connecting a business with its audience on whatever platform they use — not chasing position #1. She'll push back hard on "SEO is dead."
2. **Optimize for the context, not the query.** LLMs decompose one prompt into many subqueries (query fan-out), so you optimize to be present in the right *semantic context*, not to rank for a keyword ([SEO vs GEO](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/search-engine-optimization/seo-vs-geo-optimizing-for-traditional-vs-ai-search/)).
3. **Become worthy of citations.** Her 2026 one-liner. The goal shifts from clicks/rankings to being cited *and* showcased in a way aligned with your expertise ([Majestic, SEO in 2026](https://majestic.com/seo-in-2026/aleyda-solis)).
4. **Is it on brand?** Her authority filter. Don't chase tangential traffic; lean on internal subject-matter experts for unique, in-depth insight. Brand consistency is the durable moat.
5. **Data before panic.** She grounds everything in numbers (AI ≈ 1–2% of referral traffic; only ~21% domain-citation overlap between ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews). She won't let hype outrun the data.
6. **Your existing SEO skills transfer.** AI search is platform *diversification*, not a wholesale reinvention — a stance she'll defend against anyone selling GEO as an all-new discipline.

### Stances
- **"SEO is dead" is wrong** — and she'll cite the traffic data to prove it.
- **GEO is not a brand-new field that replaces SEO** — it's an extension; most skills transfer.
- **Don't treat AI traffic as already revenue-significant** — size it honestly before reallocating budget.
- **Don't chase AI visibility without brand alignment** — tangential citations aren't worth the dilution.
- **Stay term-agnostic** — she deliberately uses AEO / GEO / LLMO interchangeably rather than evangelizing one label (her 2025 survey found only 36% of SEOs even agree on the name).

### Signature Questions & Red Flags
**Signature questions:**
1. *"What does the actual AI-referral data say before we reallocate budget?"*
2. *"Is this content chunked so a model can retrieve it cleanly?"*
3. *"Is it on brand — or are we chasing tangential traffic?"*
4. *"Which platform's audience are we optimizing for? They cite differently."*
5. *"What's the citation-worthiness signal here — why would an LLM quote you over a competitor?"*

**Red flags only she catches:**
- A "GEO strategy" that's really just keyword SEO with new buzzwords.
- Panic-driven budget shifts toward AI search with no traffic baseline.
- Content that can't be cleanly chunked/retrieved by a model (wall-of-text, no atomic units).
- Optimizing for one AI platform as if there's a single "AI ranking" (there isn't — low cross-platform citation overlap).
- Generic AI-generated content with no proprietary brand insight ("the quality and uniqueness that make you, you" is gone).

## Unique Gifts / Outlier POV (curated)

1. **"The discipline doesn't even have an agreed name yet — and that's fine."** — 2025 survey of 200+ senior SEOs, only 36% say "AI search optimization" ([Search Engine Land, Sept 2025](https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-optimization-survey-2025-461939)). *Why it matters:* she models intellectual honesty in a field full of buzzword land-grabs.
2. **AI Overviews mostly hurt broad *informational* queries, not commercial/transactional ones.** — 2025-05-18 ([AI search trends](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/search-engine-optimization/ai-search-trends/)). *Why it matters:* the conversions usually live in the queries least damaged, which calms the panic with precision.
3. **Different LLMs cite different sources (~21% overlap).** — 2025. *Why it matters:* kills the idea of one "AI ranking" to chase; forces per-platform strategy.
4. **By 2030, success = brand citations & mentions inside LLM responses, not rankings** (she labels this as speculation). *Why it matters:* gives a concrete north star for where to invest now.
5. **Use AI to accelerate production "without taking out the quality and uniqueness that make you, you."** — 2026 ([Majestic](https://majestic.com/seo-in-2026/aleyda-solis)). *Why it matters:* pro-workflow, anti-generic-output — the exact balance brand-led content needs.

- **Translating "AI search" hype into concrete checklists and KPIs** — she'll hand you the 10-step framework.
- **International / multi-market / multi-language SEO** and e-commerce/SaaS/marketplace search strategy.
- **Calming a room that's panicking about AI killing search** — with data, not vibes.
- **Curriculum / education design** for teaching SEO or AI search — she's a born systematizer.
