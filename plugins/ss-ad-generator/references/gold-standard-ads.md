## 7 GOLD STANDARD PATTERNS

### 1. HOOK PATTERNS

**Honor Before Redirect.** Validate the audience's identity or effort BEFORE challenging it. Never lead with what they're doing wrong. Lead with what they've already earned.

**Behavioral Specificity.** "Do you talk about your HD at dinner parties?" > "Are you struggling with alignment?" Hooks that reference real behaviors create instant self-identification.

**The Uncomfortable Mirror.** Hold up a reflection the audience recognizes but hasn't articulated: "You're fine. You're functional. And something inside you is disappearing."

**Overlay as Standalone.** Test: if someone reads ONLY the overlay text on a muted video, do they stop? "Being functional is not the same as being alive" passes. "How to simplify your entire approach" does not.

### 2. BREVITY PATTERNS

**One Metaphor Per Ad.** Pick ONE analogy and ride it. Stacking metaphors loses momentum.

**Proof in 3-4 Sentences.** [Who] + [What they carried] + [What shifted] + [Visible result]. Done.

**Cut the Second Pass.** If you've said it once, trust the audience. The absence of over-explanation IS the authority.

### 3. HD INTEGRATION PATTERNS

**HD as Lens, Not Lesson.** Never teach HD in an ad. Use it as the frame: "You have a Human Design. And right now, your mind is overriding it."

**Chart-to-Body is the Universal Bridge.** The contrast between knowing your chart and feeling it in your body works every time.

**Signature Terms Over Technical Terms.** "INNERstanding" > "deconditioning."

### 4. CTA CYCLING PATTERNS

**Pattern A — Proof Bridge:** "If Darryl's story sounds like something you recognize..."
**Pattern B — Pattern Recognition:** "If that effort-first pattern feels familiar..."
**Pattern C — Curiosity:** "If you know your design but are curious how to embody it..."
**Pattern D — Direct Quiz:** "I developed a quick quiz to indicate your alignment."

**Pattern E — Risk Reversal:** "You don't need to believe me. Just come feel it." Full offer, zero commitment framing. Best for skeptic-targeted ads.

Cycle through at least 3 of 5 patterns per batch. Never use the same one more than 4x.

### 5. PROOF PATTERNS

**Named + Specific** (strongest): Full identity, numbers, visible result.
**Named + Emotional** (strong): Name but no demographics. Emotion IS the proof.
**Parable Proof** (situational): A nature/science story that demonstrates the mechanism without needing a client name. Max 1 per batch of 10.
**No Proof / Philosophy Only** (situational): When the FRAME is the proof.

Every batch of 10+ ads: at least 3 named proof stories, max 4 proof-free.

**The Credential Stack** (micro-pattern): When the practitioner's dual-world background IS the differentiator, compress 3-5 credentials into one rhythmic sentence. "Princeton engineering meets shamanic breathwork... 9D sound... NLP... and precision nervous system work." Max 1 per batch. Overuse cheapens it.

### 6. SIMPLE SHIFT PATTERNS

**The Redirection:** "Stop reading the chart. Start living in the body the chart describes."
**The Subtraction:** "Not another chart reading. A somatic activation."
**The Ratio:** "1 hour of aligned action is worth 100 hours of unconsciously grinding."

1-2 sentences max. If it takes more, it's not simple enough.

### 7. EMOTIONAL LANE PATTERNS

**Lane 1 — Identity:** "Who am I really?"
**Lane 2 — Cost Awareness:** "What is this costing me?"
**Lane 3 — Intellectual Frustration:** "Why isn't this working?"
**Lane 4 — Social Tension:** "Am I keeping up or falling behind?"
**Lane 5 — Aspiration:** "What could this feel like?"

A balanced batch includes at least one ad from each lane. Alternate push (cost/pain) and pull (aspiration/identity).
