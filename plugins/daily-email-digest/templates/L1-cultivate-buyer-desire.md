## Framework 1: The "What Not How" Framework

### Concept

One of the best ways to create desire and market your products is through a concept called "What Not How." In this kind of communication you tell them WHAT it is they're going to learn, but now HOW the result gets accomplished. The How is reserved for your program. This creates a tremendous amount of desire. It's also a great way to sell without being salesy because people feel like they've learned something. Here is an example. Notice that I'm talking about What people will learn, but the How is reserved for the program.

### Example by Joshua Sprague (2HB)

**Subject:** Key to a thriving author biz

One of the keys to having a thriving business - especially as an author - is to have multiple products that can help your audience.

Not everyone is going to purchase all of your products.

And some people are just waiting for the right product from you before they buy anything.

There are also lots of people that want your help and will buy lots of your products (as long as they're helpful).

A book is a great product.

But it's only one product.

Where do you take people next?

You can create masterclasses, coaching, challenges, courses, etc.

I'm a fan of all of those.

However, one of my absolute favorite products to create are what I call Money Making Meditations.

These simple meditations are very powerful for your audience, profitable, AND they don't take a lot of time to make.

One of my most popular meditations took me less than 30 minutes to create and it makes over $5000 a year.

That's a solid return on my time investment.

Now 5k isn't gonna let you retire, but if you have half a dozen meditations like that, it can really add up over the course of a year.

But more importantly, it's how you create and position the meditations.

Most meditations are really generic.

The way they turn into Money Making Meditations is by attaching them to specific problems your audience needs solved and knowing how to effectively launch them with ease.

Want to learn how to create, position, and launch your own meditations so they become Money Making Meditations?

That's why I created my new program *Money Making Meditations: How to Create Simple, Passive Income Meditations That Your Audience Loves (In Under An Hour).*

It's open for enrollment right now. I'm planning to bump up the price after Sunday night, April 24th.

>>Check it out right here

Talk soon,
Joshua "create multiple products" Sprague

### Practice Questions

1. What is the WHAT your audience will learn or achieve through your offer? (The result, the transformation, the outcome they want.)
2. What is the HOW that you keep reserved for your paid program? (The method, the step-by-step, the secret sauce.)
3. Can you name a specific, concrete result you or a client achieved using your method? (e.g., "took me less than 30 minutes and makes $5000/year")
4. What is the gap between what your audience is currently doing (the generic way) and what your program teaches (the specific, better way)?
5. How can you end with a clear, low-pressure call to action that connects the WHAT to your offer?

## Framework 2: "I'm thinking of this..." Email

### Concept

This is a really great type of email/communication to use when you're wanting to plant the seeds of desire for a new offer AND see if it's something that people want.

### Example by Joshua Sprague (2HB)

**Subject:** Open if you like audio meditations

Hey,

Quick question for you...

Have you ever thought about making audio meditations for your audience?

There are a lot of generic, free meditations out in the world, but creating audio meditations that address my audiences specific needs has been a great way to help them while being compensated for these audios.

Plus, they're really fast to make and profitable.

If this is something you're interested in learning about, just reply to this saying Meditations.

I'm working on something that can help you make your own powerful and profitable meditations that serve your audiences specific needs.

Anyway, if I get enough interest, then I'll put something together.

Thanks,
Joshua

P.S. This is the meditation I use every single day.

### Practice Questions

1. What new offer, product, or idea are you considering creating? Describe it in one sentence.
2. What is the core benefit your audience would get from this offer? (Keep it to one or two lines.)
3. What simple reply-trigger word could you use to gauge interest? (e.g., "Reply with MEDITATIONS," "Reply with YES")
4. How can you casually mention your own experience with this topic to build credibility without making it a hard sell?
5. What P.S. could you add that gives a taste of the value (a free resource, a personal example, a quick proof point)?

## Framework 3: Story, Takeaway, CTA (STC Email)

### Concept

This is probably the most commonly used way to create interest, intrigue, and connection. Tell a story. Share the takeaway. End with a Call to Action. The reason it works so well to create desire is because we naturally love stories. Telling a story to start draws you right into the content.

### Example by Joshua Sprague (2HB)

**Subject:** A day that would change me...

I'll never forget that day.

It was April, 2014.

I was going through a divorce and had recently moved back in with my parents in San Diego.

Things were hard. Emotionally, financially, spiritually.

I felt like such a loser.

I needed a win.

And that day, I got one.

I hung up the phone and screamed, "YES!!!"

I'd just landed my very first ghostwriting client. And it was a 5 figure deal.

I'd never sold anything for that much in my entire life, but it was a day that would change me.

I saw my value at a whole new level from then on.

I wrote the clients' book and it became a #1 Bestseller. They were thrilled.

It was the catalyst for getting me out of my divorce induced depression and led me to earning over 6 figures in ghostwriting fees.

If you love to write, then ghostwriting can be one of the most rewarding and profitable things you can do.

I'm taking a small group of people through my exact process to find, close, and fulfill high paying ghostwriting jobs.

And I'm going to show you how to become a high earning ghostwriter and land your first client within the next 10 weeks.

If you'd like to become a high earning ghostwriter (even if it's just a side gig), then reply to this email saying "FREEDOM GHOSTWRITER" and I'll explain the details.

To freedom,
Joshua Sprague

### Practice Questions

1. What is a specific turning-point moment in your life or business that changed everything? Set the scene: when, where, what was happening.
2. What were you struggling with before that moment? (Be honest and vulnerable about the low point.)
3. What was the breakthrough or win that shifted things? Describe the exact moment you felt it.
4. What is the takeaway or lesson your audience can draw from your story? How does it connect to what they want?
5. What is your call to action? How can your audience take the next step with you based on the story you just told?

## Framework 4: Story, Results, Question/CTA

### Concept

This is an iteration on an STC email. Tell a story about a student/client, share the results they got, end with "Do you want me to show you how?" or CTA.

### Example by Joshua Sprague (2HB)

**Subject:** Impossible goals

Over the years, I've become more and more fascinated by why some people get what they want in life and others don't.

Certainly people are shaped by the environment they grow up in.

Yet, I'm fascinated by the people that transcend their environment and transform their lives regardless.

It seems to me that for every 10 people that say where they grew up is the reason they don't have what they want, there are another 1 or 2 people that are getting exactly what they want despite the same circumstance.

So, what's the difference?

In my experience, it's commitment.

Commitment to their vision.

Commitment for something different.

Commitment to change no matter what.

Commitment to question the status quo.

Commitment to use their mind, heart and soul as paths to freedom instead of bondage.

Commitment to claim the desires that have been put in them by the Divine.

Commitment to let go of their stories and see things differently.

It's certainly not an easy thing to do.

But deep commitment to a higher vision changes everything.

I've not only seen this in my life, but in my clients lives repeatedly.

One of my private clients committed to create a seemingly impossible goal:

A million dollars in ten days.

It sounds bonkers to the logical mind.

That's more than she made last year.

Oh, and she's a single mom going through some pretty challenging personal circumstances.

Yet, she quantum leaped and did it in 10 days.

Then a few weeks later she did $250k in a day.

How was this possible?

Unwavering commitment. A great offer. The right support. And a willingness to work through limiting beliefs and show up regardless.

I don't take on a lot of private clients, but when it's the right fit, magic happens.

If you're interested in becoming a private client, hit reply saying "SUPPORT" and we'll see if it's right.

Either way, do yourself a favor and watch the first 90 seconds of this video on YouTube. It has a really great analogy explaining what commitment is.

Then ask yourself, how can I be more committed today?

Happy Monday,
Joshua "I'd rather create the impossible" Sprague

### Practice Questions

1. Who is a client or student whose results genuinely amazed you? What was their situation before they started working with you?
2. What specific, concrete results did they achieve? (Numbers, timelines, and tangible outcomes make this powerful.)
3. What were the key factors that made their transformation possible? (e.g., commitment, the right method, support, mindset shifts)
4. How can you frame their result as proof that your audience can do something similar?
5. What is your CTA? Do you ask them to reply, apply, or take a specific next step? How do you keep it low-pressure but clear?

### Practice Questions

1. What is something you are deeply passionate about outside of your business? Describe a specific recent experience with it.

## Framework 6: Story About Your Worldview + CTA

### Concept

Another iteration on the STC email. This time, tell a story specifically about the way you view the world, link it to a takeaway, then have a Call to Action. You can also put the Call to Action in the Postscript. I do that alot. Selling in the Postscript is a nice soft way to sell.

Do not underestimate the power of this type of email/post. It magnetizes people to you when they align with your worldview/beliefs. It is one of the most common and powerful types of communication I do.

### Example by Joshua Sprague (2HB)

**Subject:** Real talk.

Real talk... Who you are today is not who you want to be in a year. If you are the same person in a year, that's a problem. Growth is our natural cycle. Expanding our capacity is what we're made to do.

The cool thing about growth is that it's easy to measure. Is your body more fit than it was last year? Are your bank accounts fuller than last year? Are you a calmer, more loving, and more capable parent than you were last year? Are you more connected or less connected in your relationship than a year ago? Are you a better, faster, more powerful writer than you were a year ago? Do you love yourself more than you did last year?

See how easy this stuff is to measure?

Now, we might not like the answers to these questions. In fact, we might lie to ourselves about the answers and justifying why they are the way they are. But they're easy to measure. A simple yes or no will tell us the truth.

And if we will be honest enough with ourselves to look at the truth, see it for what it is, and then make the necessary changes... We will have grown into someone who has way more capacity and growth than they did a year ago.

But it starts with asking the honest questions and not sugar coating the answers. Then it takes commitment to make change. In August of 2020, I looked at myself in the mirror, and for the first time in my life I thought, "I'm fat. I need help." So, I did something I'd never done before and hired a personal trainer. I lost 30 pounds in 12 weeks and I've kept it off ever since.

But it took being honest with myself and not sugar coating anything. Then I found the right mentor and fully committed to change.

The truth will set you free.

And if you want life to look different a year from now, ask the honest questions and make the necessary changes.

With love,
Joshua "not fat anymore" Sprague

### Practice Questions

1. What is a core belief or worldview you hold that shapes how you live and work? State it plainly and directly.
2. Can you back up that belief with a personal story of transformation? What did you do, and what changed as a result?
3. How does your worldview connect to what your audience is struggling with right now? What honest questions should they be asking themselves?
4. How can you deliver a takeaway that stands on its own, even if someone never buys from you? (This is what makes worldview emails so magnetic.)
5. Can you write a P.S. that softly introduces your offer as a natural next step for anyone who resonated with the worldview you shared?

## Framework 7: "Stack The Deck" (3-Email Sequence)

### Concept

"Tease, Teach, Bite" Framework

This is a 3 part email or post framework that you can model to stack the deck in your favor so that your prospects are primed to buy your offers.

The first email teases. The second email teaches. The third email has them take an action - or "bite" onto the offer.

---

### Email #1: Tease

#### Example by Joshua Sprague (2HB)

**Subject:** Most powerful emotion in book sales?

If you're marketing or selling anything, there's one emotion that you want to cultivate more than any other. Most people don't think about creating this feeling when they're selling. Most authors rarely think about it. If you're writing a book and you intentionally create this feeling you'll have readers hungry to buy your book. And when you do release your book, people will be quick to buy a copy.

So, what is this powerful emotion? I talked about it the other day when I was being interviewed for a book marketing summit. When I started talking about the concept, I could see all of the lightbulbs start to turn on for the host.

I'll be sharing more about that interview soon, but before I tell you, let's have some fun. What do you think is the most powerful emotion you want to create to sell more of your work? Hit reply and let me know.

The first person that guesses the right answer will get a free copy of my first book. I'll tell you what the answer is tomorrow, so stay tuned...

Talk soon,
Joshua "what's your guess?" Sprague

#### Practice Questions (Tease)

1. What is a powerful concept, insight, or secret that your audience would love to know? (This is the thing you will reveal in Email #2.)
2. How can you hint at the concept without giving it away? What clues or context can you share to build curiosity?
3. Can you create an interactive element that gets replies? (e.g., "Guess the answer," "Reply with your take," a small contest or giveaway)
4. What is the promise of the reveal? ("I'll tell you tomorrow," "Stay tuned," "I'll share the answer in my next email.")

---

### Email #2: Teach

#### Example by Joshua Sprague (2HB)

**Subject:** Here it is - most powerful emotion in book sales

In yesterday's email, we had some fun. I asked you to guess the most powerful emotion to cultivate if you want to sell more of your books, products, programs, or services. There were a lot of great guesses, and close to 50 responses that came in. Yet, no one guessed the right answer. There was one response that came up over and over again that was the second most powerful emotion you can cultivate, but it wasn't the number one spot.

So, what was the second most powerful emotion if you want to sell more books? Curiosity is the second most powerful. Curiosity is important, because it gets your attention. It makes you want to know what's next. That's a really important element in marketing your books, products or services.

Yet, curiosity isn't enough. You can be curious, but not buy.

That's why the single most powerful emotion you can cultivate if you want to sell more of your books, products, programs, or services is... Desire.

Desire is so powerful. It's the gap between where your reader/customer is and where they want to be. Desire can come in a lot of ways. It can be a desire for a different financial situation, a different relationship, a relief from grief, a healthy and fit body, or any number of things. Desire is a pull inside of you to get something you don't have but you want. And when you weave that into your marketing and sales process, you will sell a lot more of your stuff with less effort.

Desire seeks relief. Desire also amplifies the more you delay gratification. That's why creating desire in your offers before you release them is so powerful.

That's one of the reasons why I teach "The Parallel Story" concept inside of The Book Marketing Masterclass. It creates desire for your book before it launches which then makes selling your book 10x easier.

But how do you create desire for all of your products, programs, or services? How do you do that in a way that makes people eager to work with you and buy your stuff? How do you become the kind of person where your customers are thinking about you even when they're not interacting with you or consuming your products? How do you become the kind of person where your customers look forward to hearing from you (and buying from you)?

Well, this email is getting a bit long to explain all of that... And do it all in a way that doesn't feel sleezy or out of integrity. I call it Buyer Desire: How to create buying desire so your customers think about you, buy from you, and tell everyone about your products.

I've never taught this stuff before and it's one of the most powerful things you can learn if you want to sell more of your work with less effort. Anyway, if you'd want me to create a new program to teach this stuff, then reply saying "DESIRE." If I get enough responses, I'll do it. If not, then I'll just keep it as my secret.

Talk soon,
Joshua "let me know" Sprague

#### Practice Questions (Teach)

1. What is the concept you teased in Email #1? Now write the full reveal: what is it, why does it matter, and why does your audience need it?
2. Can you teach the concept in a way that delivers genuine value, even if the reader never buys anything from you?
3. How can you transition from teaching into planting the seed for your offer? (Notice how Joshua goes from teaching about Desire into "I'm thinking of creating a program...")
4. What reply-trigger word can you use to gauge interest and build a warm list for the offer? (e.g., "Reply with DESIRE")

#### Practice Questions (Bite)

1. How do you reference the previous emails in the sequence so it feels like a continuation of a conversation, not a cold pitch?
2. What did your audience tell you they wanted? How can you show them you listened? (e.g., "It was a definite yes, so I'm gonna do it.")
3. How are you framing the value vs. the price? Can you make the investment feel accessible without cheapening the offer?
4. What is the format of your offer (masterclass, course, coaching, challenge)? Why did you choose that format for this audience?
5. What is the specific next step you want them to take? (Click a link, reply, join a waitlist, enroll directly.)
