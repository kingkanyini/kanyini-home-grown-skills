Your 5-Minute Texter

When someone reaches out, they hear back fast, in your voice, with your thumb on every send.

1The promise

Someone new texts your number. Within seconds they get a warm acknowledgment so they know they were heard. Moments later, a reply written in your voice is ready for you to send. The target is under five minutes, start to finish.

The honest catch: that five-minute reply depends on you. The acknowledgment is automatic, but the real reply waits for your approval. Approve quickly and it feels instant. Sit on it and the person still got the acknowledgment, just not the full reply yet. At 15 minutes the system flags the inquiry in your Airtable view and nudges you once more in Telegram with the draft, so nothing slips.

2How it keeps people safe

  • A crisis is caught before anything else. If someone sends language that signals they're in danger, the system instantly shares the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, the Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741), and 911 for immediate danger, alerts you, and steps out of that conversation so a real human (you) can step in. That message is never reworded. It exists to get help fast. And if that person reaches out again later, ready to book, they aren't locked out — their reply waits for your approval like any other, with your thumb on the send.
  • It never repeats what someone told you. By the time it writes a reply, it doesn't even have the person's words, only the safe facts you approved.
  • It only says what's on your FAQ. No improvised prices, hours, or advice. Anything outside the lines routes to you.
  • Nothing sends without you. You read and approve every reply.

3What you own

This isn't a seat you rent in someone else's tool. The engine runs in your n8n account, on your phone number, with your data in your Airtable. If you ever want to change it, pause it, or walk away with it, it's yours.
