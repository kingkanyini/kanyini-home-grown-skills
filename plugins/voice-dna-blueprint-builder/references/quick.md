## Pre-Bank Setup

### Conversational Contract (read verbatim before Q1)

> Before we start — I'm going to ask you 5 questions about the edges of who you are: what you push against, what you'd die on, the things that make you, you. I'm a structured listener, not a therapist — I'll keep us moving, reflect back what I hear, and won't push past anything you'd rather skip. You can skip any question, end early, or ask me to rephrase. Your answers go into a transcript and a Voice DNA Blueprint saved only to your machine. Ready when you are.

### Runtime Wrapper (prefix to every question)

> Take your time with this — there's no word limit. List as many as come up.

## Questions (linear presentation order)

### Q1: IDENTITY

What are some unusual habits or absurd things that you love? Give me a few — the weirder, the better.

*Source: adapted from Tim Ferriss, Tribe of Mentors Q6 (pluralized)*

---

### Q2: PILLARS

If you had a few gigantic billboards getting seen by millions or billions of people — what would they each say, and why those messages specifically?

*Source: adapted from Tim Ferriss, Tribe of Mentors Q4 (pluralized)*

---

### Q3: CONNECTION

What are some of the things people have said to you recently that made you feel really seen? What were they responding to, and what made each one land?

*Source: drafted (Greyling LLM lens + Tannen scene-construction)*

---

### Q4: ORIGIN

What are some of the failures — or apparent failures — that set you up for who you are now? Do you have a favorite among them, and why that one?

*Source: adapted from Tim Ferriss, Tribe of Mentors Q3 (plural + "favorite" magic word preserved per Ferriss reviewer edit)*

---

### Q5: LANGUAGE

What are some of the words or phrases everyone in your space uses that just make you cringe? Name a few — and walk me through why each one lands wrong for you.

*Source: drafted (Greyling LLM-anti-sycophancy + Tannen LANGUAGE lens)*
