# Goodbye / Final Email — Reference Example (Gold Standard)

> **Source:** Business Bottleneck Quiz pre-quel E2 — the ~72h last-call / final abandonment email. 2026-07-09. <your-name>-authored + rewritten.
> **Subject:** Goodbye
> **Preheader:** Is it me leaving, or you letting go?
> **When to load:** any FINAL / goodbye / last-call / "breakup" email that closes a sequence or an abandonment set (e.g., SOS Email 5, the final reminder in a nurture set).
> **Why this is the gold standard:**
> - **Double-meaning subject** — "Goodbye" reads two ways at once: the sender stepping back, AND the reader getting to say goodbye to their blockage by saying yes. The body pays off both.
> - **Goodbye-as-throughline open** — they were already ready to say goodbye to a capped life; not following through is why the sender now says goodbye to them. No greeting; opens on the punch.
> - **Reframe with depth** — "why I call it an adventure": go inward, discover something, let something go, activate a superpower on the far side. "Business is a spiritual practice" makes the mirror explicit (the bottleneck in your company is the bottleneck in you).
> - **Priority tell** — we're never "too busy" for what we truly want (kids, pets, ourselves), so avoidance signals a stuck part. Names the resistance without shaming.
> - **Shadow/resistance layer** — "Resistance shows up loudest right before your next breakthrough."
> - **Honest-cost close** — the only urgency is their own lost time, never manufactured scarcity. Invite stays open ("no countdown, no expiry").
> - **Agency return** — "One goodbye ends it. You get to choose whose."
> - Voice hygiene: zero em dashes, one CTA, 3-dot ellipsis.

---

Saying goodbye isn't easy, but it's necessary.

For example...

When you decided to break through your business's bottleneck, you were ready to say goodbye.

To say goodbye to a way of life where your business is capped.

But since you haven't followed through with that step, it tells me I have to say goodbye to you.

Not the dramatic kind. This is just the last time I'll remind you to activate your "Adventure Audit."

But before I go, let me tell you why I call the Business Bottleneck Breakthrough Quiz an "Adventure Audit."

Because it was never a quiz. It's an adventure inward.

You go in to discover something, the real thing that's been capping you. You let something go, the block that's been guarding it. And on the far side of it, you activate a superpower you forgot you had.

Business is a spiritual practice. The bottleneck in your company is almost always the bottleneck within you. Same block. Same doorway. Cross it in one, and you cross it in both.

Which brings me to the truth many never figure out.

Something in you wanted this. You felt what keeps your business tethered to you, and you went looking to name it.

Then three days passed, and you still haven't finished.

Sit with that for a second.

That hesitation might not be a scheduling problem. "I'll get to it later" rarely is.

This is because we prioritize what is important to us.

We are never "too busy" to feed our kids, or our pets, maybe ourselves...

But you get the point.

You prioritize those things that you deeply desire. And if you aren't ready to face the blockage in your business, then some part of you is stuck.

That's not a knock. Why? Because...

Resistance shows up loudest right before your next breakthrough. It always has.

So here's the goodbye that actually matters. It was never mine to say.

It's yours.

Fifteen minutes, one honest yes, and you say goodbye to what's kept you the bottleneck. Goodbye to being the one it all waits on. Goodbye to the version of this that never changes.

That's the far side. Your genius, unblocked, finally free to build.
