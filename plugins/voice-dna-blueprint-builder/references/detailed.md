# Voice DNA Blueprint Builder — DETAILED Bank (10q)

**Tier:** Detailed
**Length:** 10 questions (Quick 5 + 5 more)
**Estimated time:** ~30 minutes
**Use case:** Comprehensive intake when you need richer texture than Quick but lighter than full In-Depth
**Counsel-reviewed:** 2026-05-22 — passed at avg 9.10/10 (Voice DNA Interview Research Counsel #50)

---

## Pre-Bank Setup

### Conversational Contract (read verbatim before Q1)

> Before we start — I'm going to ask you 10 questions about the edges of who you are: what you push against, what you'd die on, the things that make you, you. I'm a structured listener, not a therapist — I'll keep us moving, reflect back what I hear, and won't push past anything you'd rather skip. You can skip any question, end early, or ask me to rephrase. Your answers go into a transcript and a Voice DNA Blueprint saved only to your machine. Ready when you are.

### Runtime Wrapper (prefix to every question)

> Take your time with this — there's no word limit. List as many as come up.

---

## Questions (linear presentation order)

### Q1: IDENTITY

What are some unusual habits or absurd things that you love? Give me a few — the weirder, the better.

*Source: adapted from Tim Ferriss, Tribe of Mentors Q6*

---

### Q2: PILLARS

If you had a few gigantic billboards getting seen by millions or billions of people — what would they each say, and why those messages specifically?

*Source: adapted from Tim Ferriss, Tribe of Mentors Q4*

---

### Q3: CONNECTION

What are some of the things people have said to you recently that made you feel really seen? What were they responding to, and what made each one land?

*Source: drafted (Greyling + Tannen)*

---

### Q4: ORIGIN

What are some of the failures — or apparent failures — that set you up for who you are now? Do you have a favorite among them, and why that one?

*Source: adapted from Tim Ferriss, Tribe of Mentors Q3*

---

### Q5: LANGUAGE

What are some of the words or phrases everyone in your space uses that just make you cringe? Name a few — and walk me through why each one lands wrong for you.

*Source: drafted (Greyling + Tannen)*

---

### Q6: IDENTITY

What are some things that are true about how you actually work — the day-to-day reality — that would surprise the people who only see the polished version?

*Source: drafted (Bartlett "what people don't know" lens + Tannen scene-construction)*

---

### Q7: PILLARS

What are the frameworks or principles you'd die on a hill for? Tell me about a recent time one of them came under pressure and you held the line.

*Source: drafted (Tannen narrative-mode rewrite of "non-negotiable" prompt — counsel edit applied to defuse analytical-mode and gendered double-bind risk)*

---

### Q8: CONNECTION

When you think of the word *successful*, who are the people who come to mind first — and what about each of them makes you call them that?

*Source: adapted from Tim Ferriss Show Ep #284 (pluralized)*

---

### Q9: ORIGIN

Take me back to some of the moments that made you who you are today. What were the befores and afters?

*Source: drafted (Deibel/Evanhoe CxD before/after structure)*

---

### Q10: LANGUAGE

When you're really in the flow talking about your work, what are some of the words or phrases that keep coming out of your mouth — and what do you think they're pointing to about how you actually see things?

*Source: drafted (Tannen involvement-state lens)*
