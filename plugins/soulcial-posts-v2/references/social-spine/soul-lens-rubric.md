# Soulcial Media Engine — Soul-Lens Intensity Rubric (H5)

**Canonical source for:** P2, P6 (direct references), and any phase where the soul-lens dial shapes output.
**Set in Phase 0.** Referenced by `phases-1-7.md` and the Stage 2 Vex King agent review.

The soul-lens dial controls how explicitly the brand's conscious/values layer surfaces in copy. It is not a binary on/off — it is a calibrated intensity setting with concrete output deltas.

---

## Core Rubric: 4 Axes × 3 Settings

| Axis | Subtle | Balanced | Overt |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spiritual vocabulary** | None explicit — values expressed through word choice and story, no spiritual terms in copy | Light — one or two conscious/values words per piece (e.g., "presence," "intention") without defining them | Foregrounded — spiritual vocabulary named and used as the lens (e.g., "energy," "healing," "subtle realm," "consciousness") |
| **Framework naming** (your named frameworks) | Off — no signature framework terms appear in public copy | One per piece — one framework term introduced with brief context, never stacked | Woven through — multiple framework names used across the piece; audience is assumed to know the vocabulary |
| **Story-vs-statement ratio** | Story-led, value implied — the story carries the lesson; the principle is never stated outright | Mixed — one explicit principle statement embedded in a story arc; story still does the heavy lifting | Principle stated then illustrated — the conscious insight lands first, the story serves as proof |
| **CTA tone** | Invitation — soft, curiosity-led ("if this landed for you…") | Gentle direct — clear ask with warmth ("drop a comment / save this") | Mission-framed — the CTA is tied to a larger purpose or transformation ("this is how we change the game together") |

---

## Ceiling Guardrail (Vex Re-Gate)

> **Overt ≠ preachy. No spiritual-bypassing.**
>
> Even at full overt intensity, the copy must **validate the feeling before reframing it.** Never skip straight to the insight. Never imply the audience should already be beyond what they're feeling.
>
> Voice rule: "you may feel broken, but…" — not "you're not broken." Validate first, then reframe.
>
> **Test before shipping at Overt:** read the copy as someone mid-struggle. Does it feel like it *sees* them first, or does it hand them a principle to fix themselves? If it fixes before it sees — revise.

This row is **non-bypassable.** Vex King's Stage 2 agent review applies it as a blocker-level check on P2 and P6 output.

## Worked Example: The Same Post at Three Settings

**Source idea:** "Most people quit because they don't feel seen — not because they lack discipline."

---

### Subtle setting

> Discipline isn't the problem. Most people stop because no one noticed they started.
>
> If you've been putting in the work without recognition, keep going. The evidence compounds before anyone else can see it.

*(Values: consistency, inner authority. No spiritual vocab. No framework names. Invitation CTA implied, not stated.)*

---

### Balanced setting

> Most people don't quit from lack of discipline. They quit because they feel invisible.
>
> There's a difference between the grind and the inner game. When you practice *presence* with your own progress — noticing the small wins before anyone else does — momentum stops depending on external validation.
>
> Save this one. You'll need it on the quiet weeks.

*(One conscious word: "presence." Mixed story/principle. Gentle direct CTA.)*

---

### Overt setting

> You didn't lose discipline. You lost connection to your **IR Station** — the internal console where your real momentum lives.
>
> Most people are trying to level up with their eyes on someone else's leaderboard. But your YOUniverse runs on a different map. When you stop playing for the crowd and start playing for the growth, the "I should quit" voice loses its volume.
>
> This is the work. Drop a "🎮" below if you're in it — let's make sure you're not playing alone.

*(Framework names: IR Station, YOUniverse. Principle stated first. Mission-framed CTA. Ceiling check: the opening validates feeling disconnected before offering the reframe.)*

## Usage Notes

- P2 and P6 read this rubric directly when generating output.
- The setting is locked in Phase 0 and held in `00-master-brief.md` under `soul_lens_intensity`.
- When in doubt, default to **balanced** — it preserves optionality and reads cleanly across audiences who may not know the user's frameworks yet.
- **Client mode:** always ask the client which setting fits their brand before writing. Never assume overt for a non-spiritually-positioned brand.
