# Platform Specs (v2)

Per-platform character limits, hook windows, hashtag rules, the algorithm-reward → CTA map, and the CTA menus. Any writer skill sizes to the target platform, then picks the CTA that drives the metric that platform actually rewards.

## Platform Constraints

| Platform | Max length | Hook window | Hashtags | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------------|----------|-------|
| **X / Twitter** | 280 chars | First 5-10 words | **None** | Best range is 60-100 chars. A link in the body chokes reach, so save it for the final tweet of a thread. |
| **Threads** | 500 chars | First line | **None** | Replies drive it, just like X. |
| **Bluesky** | 300 chars | First line | **None** | — |
| **LinkedIn** | ~3,000 chars | Opening 2 lines (about 140 chars, until "…see more" cuts it off) | 3-5 optional at end | Aim for 1,200-1,500 chars. Keep outside links out of the post and drop them in the first comment. |
| **Instagram (feed)** | 2,200 chars | First 125 chars | 3-5 niche tags at end | Needs an image or video. Only one CTA. |
| **Instagram (Reels)** | caption 2,200 chars | On-screen text in first 1.7s | 3-5 niche tags | Watch-through rules everything. Put the hook on screen, not only in the caption. |
| **Facebook** | long allowed | First 40-80 chars optimal | **None** | Bait like "comment YES" gets the post throttled. |
| **TikTok caption** | ~2,200 chars (keep <150) | Keyword in first 30 chars; spoken hook in first 1.7s | 5 max | Lives and dies on watch-time and completion. |
| **Substack Notes** | short/punchy | First line | **None** | Restacks drive it. Write a line people want to screenshot. |

**Multi-platform rule:** draft the version for the roomiest platform first, then offer a trimmed cut for the tight ones (X / Bluesky). One length pasted everywhere never fits. Every platform gets its own native size.

## Algorithm-Reward → CTA Map

Every platform pays out on its own signal. Choose the CTA that earns that signal. One ask per post, never a stack of them.

| Platform | Signal it rewards most | CTA that earns it |
|----------|--------------|---------------|
| **LinkedIn** | Comments (count ~2x a like) | A question that splits the room · "What's missing from this?" |
| **Instagram (feed)** | Saves, then shares | "Bookmark this for [moment]" · "Pass this to [person]" |
| **Instagram (Reels)** | Completion, then saves | Hook as on-screen text + "Save it for later" |
| **Facebook** | Shares, saves | "Share this with [the person it's for]" |
| **X / Twitter** | Replies (count ~75x a like) | A take people will argue with · "Change my mind" |
| **Threads** | Replies | Same as X |
| **TikTok** | Watch-time / completion | Spoken hook inside 1.7s · on-screen tease ("stay for the end") |
| **Substack Notes** | Restacks (= shares) | One sharp line worth screenshotting |

## CTA Menus

### Share-driving CTAs (strongest — trigger the metric algorithms reward most)
- "Tag the one friend who [specific situation]." (shares + tags)
- "Bookmark this for [the day they'll reach for it]." (saves)
- "DM this to someone who [specific trait]." (DMs)
- "Screenshot it. Keep it where you'll see it tomorrow." (saves + shares)

### Comment-driving CTAs (best for LinkedIn, X, Threads)
- "What [specific thing] would you put on this list?"
- "Is it just me, or does this happen to you too?"
- "Change my mind." (polarizing)
- A two-sided question the reader can't answer without choosing one.

### DM-driving CTAs (best for Instagram, Facebook lead gen)
- "Drop '[KEYWORD]' in the comments and the [specific resource] is yours."
- "Send me '[KEYWORD]' and I'll share my [thing]."
- Keyword asks like these fire DM automations and build the list.

### Weak CTAs to avoid
- "Thoughts?" (so open that nobody answers)
- "Smash that like button!" (begging, and algorithms push it down)
- "Link in bio" (dead unless you've given a real reason to go)
- "Drop a comment below" (passive, gives them nothing to say)

## Universal voice rules (pre-grade checklist)
- [ ] Contracted forms (can't, we're, you'll, isn't)
- [ ] Em dashes handled per the active voice profile (zero by default; allowed only if the profile documents em-dashes-on-social as a tic)
- [ ] Digits for numbers ("3 steps," not "three steps")
- [ ] No passive constructions anywhere
- [ ] None of the padding words (actually, basically, just, literally, really, very) unless the profile lists one as a tic
- [ ] One post, one concrete idea
- [ ] Sounds like a person talking, not a filled-in template
