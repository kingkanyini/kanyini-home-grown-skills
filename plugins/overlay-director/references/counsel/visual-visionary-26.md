## What this counsel watches for
- **Rhythm over density** — variety_score is necessary, not sufficient; does the *sequence* breathe?
- **Mobile legibility** — hold long enough to read; dark plate behind art captions (principle 6); no ≥3 cards at once.
- **Cam-clearance** — cards shifted off the face unless they're frame-centered by design (principle 5).
- **Anchoring** — every card tied to a spoken moment, lead-in before the word lands (principle 4).
- **Authored, not generated** — the whole set reads like one hand made deliberate choices.

## Required outputs per review (CLAUDE.md Counsel Review Behavior — never rubber-stamp)
For each card (per-card rubric) AND for the sequence (sequence rubric):
- **Score** (1–10) — calibrated: 0.90+ = top 10% of all overlay work, not just this video.
- **What lands** — a specific card/beat/technique that works (quote the moment + timestamp).
- **What's weak** — a specific weakness.
- **Edit suggestion** — a concrete, line-level change (which card, what to do).

After all members review, synthesize the strongest notes into a revision; integrate minority
dissent as hardening conditions (per `principles/dissent-integration-synthesis`). The job is
to PUSH the build forward, not approve it.
