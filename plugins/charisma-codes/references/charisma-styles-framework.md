## Overview

Each person has THREE Charisma Style dimensions. Within each dimension, they are ONE sub-style:

- **ONE Energetic Style (Yellow)** — The fun part of your charisma
- **ONE Trust Style (Blue)** — The empathetic part of your charisma
- **ONE Authority Style (Red)** — The in charge part of your charisma

Your specific combination is your **Charisma Style Recipe.**
That is 6 x 3 x 3 = **54 unique combinations.**

### <your-name>'s Coaching Layer (added depth, not replacement)

- **Energetic** = Your physical energy signature on camera — what people SEE and HEAR first (pitch, pacing, emphasis, arms, face)
- **Trust** = How you communicate to build trust — your focus (behavior/emotions/thoughts) and delivery style
- **Authority** = How you lead and establish authority — your motivational approach, testimonial style, and vibe

**Arms:** B = Big, Fl = Fluid, Sm = Small, Sh = Sharp

## DIAGNOSTIC LOGIC REFERENCE

### The Wrong-Style Diagnostic Engine

This is the core validation mechanic. It uses the consequence matrices above to confirm or pivot a proposed Charisma Code.

### How It Works

**Step 1 — Present consequences for the proposed style.**
For each dimension (Energetic, Trust, Authority), look at the row matching the proposed style. Read across every column except the diagonal. These are the consequences of that person using the WRONG style.

**Step 2 — Ask which consequences feel familiar.**
Present the consequences as a list. Ask: "Which of these have you experienced? Which feel like feedback you have actually gotten — or energy you have noticed landing wrong?"

**Step 3 — Evaluate the response.**

- **If they recognize consequences from their OWN row:** Style CONFIRMED. The mismatches they experience are consistent with someone of that style drifting into other styles.
- **If they recognize a consequence from a DIFFERENT row:** Pivot investigation needed. See Step 4.

**Step 4 — Pivot logic (the detective work).**

When a user recognizes a consequence that does NOT appear in their proposed row, trace it back:

1. Identify the consequence word (e.g., "BORING")
2. Identify the column it was presented under (e.g., the CHARM column)
3. Scan the FULL column for that consequence word
4. Find which ROW contains that consequence in that column
5. That row is the candidate for their ACTUAL style

**Step 5 — Walk through transparently.**
Always explain the logic to the user. Never silently pivot.

### Worked Example

> User is proposed as **CHARM** (Energetic).
> They say: "People seem bored by me sometimes."
>
> Look at the Energetic consequence matrix, CHARM column:
> - AMAZE row + CHARM column = MEAN
> - EXCITE row + CHARM column = MEAN
> - CHARM row = (you) — diagonal, skip
> - PERFORM row + CHARM column = **BORING**
> - IMPRESS row + CHARM column = MEAN
> - ROAR row + CHARM column = MEAN
>
> BORING appears in the PERFORM row under the CHARM column.
> This means: A PERFORM person using CHARM energy looks BORING.
> Conclusion: The user might actually be **PERFORM**, not CHARM.
> Investigate PERFORM — present the PERFORM row consequences and re-check.

### Multiple Recognition Handling

If a user recognizes consequences from multiple rows, prioritize the row where they recognize the MOST consequences. Use follow-up questions to narrow. Two strong hits from one row beats one hit each from two rows.

### Confidence Levels

- **HIGH:** User recognizes 3+ consequences from their proposed row across all dimensions
- **MEDIUM:** User recognizes 1-2, with no contradictory signals from other rows
- **LOW:** User recognizes consequences from a different row, or gives vague/uncertain answers — pivot and re-test

## STYLE INTERACTION NOTES

### Cross-Dimensional Patterns

Some style combinations create natural synergy; others create internal tension worth coaching around:

- High-energy Energetic styles (EXCITE, PERFORM, ROAR) paired with STEADY Trust can feel like whiplash — coach smooth transitions
- IMPRESS Energetic with LIGHT Authority can feel contradictory (slow energy + party promoter vibe) — coach intentional gear shifts
- CHARM Energetic with LEAD Authority is a power combination — warmth plus directness
- AMAZE Energetic with MIRROR Trust creates deep relatability — genuine wonder plus emotional reflection

These are coaching observations, not framework rules. The framework itself is style-agnostic on combinations.
