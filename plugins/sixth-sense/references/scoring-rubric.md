# SixthSense Scoring Rubric

## Score Tiers

| Tier | Range | Meaning | Expected Frequency |
|------|-------|---------|--------------------|
| **GOLD** | 0.90 - 1.00 | Top 10% of ALL social media clips ever seen, not just this video. Immediate publish candidate. | 1-3 per scan |
| **STRONG** | 0.80 - 0.89 | High-quality clip. Needs minor editing or context framing. Worth producing. | 5-8 per scan |
| **SOLID** | 0.70 - 0.79 | Good raw material. Needs editing, overlay work, or extended window. | 5-10 per scan |
| **REFERENCE** | < 0.70 | Background material. May feed into compilations or longer content. Not standalone. | Remainder |

## Calibration Anchor (ALL AGENTS)

> **0.90+ (GOLD) means this segment would perform in the top 10% of ALL social media clips you have ever seen, regardless of creator. Do NOT give GOLD scores simply because it is the best segment in THIS video. A mediocre video may have ZERO gold segments. A great video may have 3-4.**

## Scanner Scoring Dimensions

### P1-A: Content Scanner (5 dimensions)

| Dimension | Weight | What It Measures |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Teaching Value | 25% | Does this teach something actionable? Framework, principle, technique? |
| Quotability | 20% | Does it contain a phrase someone would screenshot or repeat? |
| Story Arc | 20% | Does it have a beginning, middle, and end within the segment? |
| Aha Patterns | 15% | Does it create an "aha moment" — a reframe, paradox, or insight? |
| Clip Target Alignment | 20% | Does the segment length match a requested clip target? |

### P2-A: Emotion Scanner (4 dimensions)

| Dimension | Weight | What It Measures |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Vulnerability Depth | 30% | How raw and authentic is the emotional exposure? |
| Goosebump Factor | 25% | Does it trigger a visceral emotional response? |
| Energy Activation | 25% | Does the energy map confirm an emotional shift? (BPM, silence %, bars) |
| Audience Resonance | 20% | Will the target audience feel seen/recognized/moved? |

### P3-A: Structure Scanner (5 dimensions)

| Dimension | Weight | What It Measures |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Hook Strength | 25% | Does the first 3-5 seconds stop the scroll? |
| Entry/Exit Quality | 25% | Can the clip start and end cleanly without context? |
| Pacing | 20% | Does it build, maintain rhythm, and avoid dead air? |
| Loop Potential | 15% | Does the end connect back to the beginning? |
| Platform Fit | 15% | Does the length and format match target platforms? |

## Deep Diver Adjustment Bands

| Agent | Adjustment Range | Rationale |
|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| P1-B (Content Deep Diver) | +/- 0.05 | 7-point analysis; moderate new information |
| P2-B (Emotion Deep Diver) | +/- 0.05 | 9-point analysis; energy correlation may shift score |
| P3-B (Structure Deep Diver) | +/- 0.07 | 12-dimension analysis reveals significantly more than Scanner's 5 dimensions |

## Consensus Score Adjustments

| Flag Count | Adjustment | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|-----------|
| Triple-flagged (all 3 lenses) | Average of 3 adjusted scores | Maximum confidence; no discount |
| Double-flagged (2 of 3 lenses) | Average of 2 scores, then -0.02 | Minor discount for missing lens |
| Single-flagged (1 lens only) | Adjusted score -0.05 | Discount for narrow signal |
| Emotion-Only Discovery | No discount (use P2-B adjusted score) | Emotion-only moments are features, not gaps |

## Emotion-Only Promotion Rule

Emotion-Only Discoveries scoring 0.85+ on P2-B adjusted score are promoted directly to the Consensus Map. These are segments the Content and Structure Scanners missed but the Emotion Scanner caught — they represent unique emotional peaks that may be the most powerful clips in the set.
