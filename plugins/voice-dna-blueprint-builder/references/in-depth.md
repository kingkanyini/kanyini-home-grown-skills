## Synthesis-Time Orchestrator Instructions

Before generating the Voice DNA Blueprint from the captured answers, the orchestrator MUST:

1. **List all 15 Qs with verbatim answers in two columns** — forces re-attention to middle turns and prevents Lost-in-Middle (per Greyling research). This is the v1 mitigation for not having mid-flow consolidation gates.
2. **Cross-reference each blueprint section to source Qs** — every claim in the blueprint must be anchored to a verbatim Q&A reference.
3. **Flag any blueprint section that draws disproportionately from Qs 1-2 or 14-15 only** — that signals middle-turn ghosting; surface to user as a quality flag.

---

## Failure Signals to Watch For (trigger v1.1 chunking addition if observed)

- Blueprints under-quoting middle Qs (5-10)
- Subjects asking "did you hear the thing I said about X" mid-interview
- Verbatim recovery strong from openers/closers but middle paraphrased only
- 2+ runs showing these signals → add mid-interview consolidation gate in v1.1
