# Headline Workshop Protocol

*Activated during Phase 2 when auditing the Hero section. This is a sub-flow, not a standalone skill.*

---

## When to Activate
- Hero section audit
- Any section where the headline needs a rewrite (not just a tweak)
- When counsel flags RED on a headline

## IMPORTANT: Workshop the Full Hero Stack
The headline is not just the H1. The real unit is the **entire Hero section** working together:
- H1 (main headline)
- H2 (sub-headline)
- Bridge copy (description paragraph)
- Identity hook (if present)
- CTA button text + sub-CTA
- Urgency line

Workshop the full stack as a unit. Present options as complete stacks, not isolated H1s. The user will often propose a full rewrite — review the whole thing together, don't force them to lock H1 before moving to H2.

## Prerequisites
- Client's OO must be loaded
- Client's Voice DNA (if available)
- Current **full Hero section** captured verbatim (not just the headline)

---

## Step 1: OO Data Extraction

Pull headline ingredients from OO:

| Ingredient | OO Section | Example |
|------------|-----------|---------|
| **Avatar identity** | Sec 2 (Client Snapshot) | "High-achieving woman, entrepreneur, former corporate" |
| **Core pain** | Sec 3 (Core Pain) | "Self-abandonment disguised as functionality" |
| **Point B outcome** | Sec 4 (Point B) | "Embodied self-trust, creative aliveness" |
| **Promise** | Sec 7 (Promise) | "Embodied self-trust" |
| **Big desire** | Sec 8 (Big Desire) | "Liberated. Authentic. Alive." |
| **Fears** | Sec 10 (Fears) | "Never feeling fully alive again" |
| **Mechanism** | Sec 11 (New Method) | "Somatic creative activation" |

---

## Step 2: Generate Headline Options

Generate 5-7 headline options using these formulas:

### Formula 1: Paradox Hook
`[External Success] Yet [Internal Gap]?`
- Both sides OO-grounded
- Creates intrigue by naming a contradiction the avatar lives with
- Best for high-ticket ($3,000+)

### Formula 2: How To + OO
`How To [OO Sec 4/7 Result] Without [OO Sec 3/10 Fear]`
- Classic Russell Brunson formula populated with OO data
- Works for any price point

### Formula 3: Self-Identification
`For [OO Sec 2 Avatar] Who [OO Sec 3 Situation]`
- Direct call-out of the avatar
- Filters instantly — right people lean in, wrong people bounce

### Formula 4: Question + Paradox
`What If [OO Sec 10 Fear] Was Actually [OO Sec 7 Promise]?`
- Reframes the fear as the doorway
- Works well for transformation offers

### Formula 5: Outcome Statement
`[OO Sec 8 Big Desire] — Starting With [OO Sec 11 Mechanism]`
- Leads with what they want, follows with how
- Clean and direct

### Formula 6: Desire + Fear + Time Compression
`[What They Desire Most] Without [What They Fear Most]` — optionally add a compressed timeline.
- The core is: **desire on one side, fear on the other**
- Can take many forms: "The Key To [Desire] Without [Fear]?", "How To [Desire] Without [Fear]", or just "[Desire]. Without [Fear]."
- Adding a time element ("in 8 weeks", "in 90 days") compresses the decision window
- The phrasing should match the client's voice — "The Key To" creates a curiosity question, "How To" is more instructional
- Populate BOTH sides from OO: Desire = OO Sec 4/7/8, Fear = OO Sec 3/10
- Example: "The Key To Breaking Through Without Burning Out?" (example-collective — desire from Sec 4, fear from Sec 10)

---

## Step 3: Counsel Scoring

Each counsel member scores each headline on:

| Criteria | Weight | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| **OO Alignment** | 30% | How deeply does it reflect the OO data? Cite sections. |
| **Avatar Resonance** | 25% | Would the OO avatar stop scrolling? Self-identification? |
| **Micro-Arc** | 20% | Does it hit camaraderie + curiosity in one line? |
| **Voice Match** | 15% | Does it sound like the CLIENT, not generic marketing? |
| **Premium Signal** | 10% | Does the language match the price point? |

Present scores in a table. Each counsel member must cite OO sections for their scoring.

### Counsel Vote Protocol (for 3+ options)
When <your-name> asks the counsel to vote on multiple options (or when there are 3+ strong contenders):

1. **Each counsel member evaluates EVERY option** — score (X/10) + reasoning with OO citations
2. **Present as a vote matrix:**
```
| Option | Russell | Myron | Marisa | Average |
|--------|---------|-------|--------|---------|
| A      | 8       | 8     | 7.5    | 7.8     |
| B      | 8.5     | 9     | 8.5    | 8.7     |
| C      | 9.5     | 9     | 9.5    | 9.3     |
```
3. **Declare winner** by: highest average score + most first-place votes. If split, note the split and let <your-name> break the tie.
4. **<your-name>'s instinct still overrides** — the vote informs, it doesn't decide.

This protocol can be invoked during any Phase 2 decision, not just headline workshops. Any time multiple copy options are being compared, the counsel vote protocol applies.

---

## Step 4: <your-name>'s Instinct

Present the top 3 scored headlines. <your-name> picks or modifies. Counsel's job is done — <your-name>'s instinct drives the final choice.

If <your-name> wants a hybrid of two options, merge them and run a quick counsel check on the merged version.

**When <your-name> proposes their own headline direction:** Counsel MUST explore it before scoring it down. The user often sees the avatar from a different angle than counsel. Have counsel articulate WHY the user's direction works (not just why it doesn't). If counsel initially dismisses an idea and <your-name> pushes back, counsel reconsiders — the pushback is data, not stubbornness.

---

## Key Rules
- NEVER use generic copywriting formulas without OO data
- Headlines land on OUTCOMES, not mechanisms
- The paradox hook is the strongest pattern for high-ticket transformation offers
- Voice DNA must be cross-referenced if available
- Max 3 rounds of revision — expect iteration. If it's not clicking after 3 rounds, step back and re-examine the OO data
- **Workshop the full Hero stack** — never lock H1 in isolation. The H1, H2, bridge, identity hook, CTA, and urgency line are one unit.
- **User's direction first** — if <your-name> proposes a headline, counsel explores it before offering alternatives
