# Perfect Webinar LITE Framework

*Adapted from Russell Brunson's Perfect Webinar for HTML presentation decks. Reference for Agent 2 (Content Architect).*

---

## THE ARC (Every Webinar Follows This)

```
HOOK → PROBLEM → STORY → SOLUTION → PROOF → STACK → CLOSE
```

### 1. HOOK (Slides 1-3)
**Purpose:** Pattern interrupt. Stop the scroll. Earn the next 5 minutes.

- **Title slide:** The big promise. "How To [RESULT] Without [PAIN] In [TIMELINE]"
- **What you'll learn:** Set expectations. "During this presentation you'll see..."
- **Ethics/Humor:** Light moment (Darth Vader "don't use for evil" or similar)

**Emotional Pillar:** CURIOSITY — open a loop they need closed.

### 2. PROBLEM (Slides 4-6)
**Purpose:** Name the pain. Make them feel seen.

- **Who is this for:** Qualify the audience. Include AND exclude.
- **Objection handling:** "What if I'm not [X]?" — dissolve the biggest barrier early.
- **Why most [X] fail:** Name 3 specific reasons their current approach isn't working.

**Emotional Pillar:** RELIEF — "finally, someone understands my situation."

### 3. STORY (Slides 7-9)
**Purpose:** Credibility through vulnerability. YOU were once where THEY are.

- **Why listen to me:** Credentials without bragging. Let achievements speak.
- **The turning point:** What happened that changed everything.
- **Who you learned from:** Name your mentors. Shows investment + humility.

**Emotional Pillar:** CAMARADERIE — "this person is like me, but further along."

### 4. SOLUTION (Slides 10-14)
**Purpose:** Reveal the mechanism. Show WHY your approach works.

- **The discovery:** What clicked. The "aha" moment.
- **The framework:** Your unique mechanism (6 Core Beliefs, 3 Secrets, etc.)
- **The journey:** Timeline showing the process (visual — treasure map, timeline, etc.)
- **Your role vs. their role:** What they bring vs. what you build.
- **What gets built:** Visual grid of all deliverables.

**Emotional Pillar:** CURIOSITY + RELIEF — "this is a SYSTEM, not another hustle."

### 5. PROOF (Slide 15)
**Purpose:** Show, don't tell. Let results speak.

- **Live demo:** "Let me show you what this looks like..." → switch to real funnel/product
- This is where you SHOW the finished product, not just talk about it.

### 6. STACK (Slides 16-19)
**Purpose:** Build perceived value before revealing price.

- **Progressive stack:** Each slide adds a new tier. Running total grows.
  - Slide 16: Tier 1 (items + value = $X)
  - Slide 17: Tier 1 + 2 (previous grayed, new bold, total = $Y)
  - Slide 18: Tier 1 + 2 + 3 (collapsed + new, total = $Z)
  - Slide 19: Full package (collapsed + new, total = $TOTAL)

**Between stack slides:** Story or proof interludes (Russell Brunson pattern).

**Key principle:** Each tier name should feel like a LEVEL UP, not just "more features."

### 7. CLOSE (Slides 20-25)
**Purpose:** Create the decision moment. Two paths. Price reveal. Action.

**Close Sequence (Short/25-slide template):**
1. **"If All I Did Was..."** (Slide 20) — 3 angles:
   - Revenue: "If all I did was help you [specific revenue outcome]..."
   - Automation: "If all I did was automate [specific pain]..."
   - Identity: "If all I did was build you a system for [lifestyle outcome]..."
   - Each ends with: "...would it be worth it?"

2. **Two Paths** (Slide 21) — The choice:
   - Path 1 (gray): Keep doing what you're doing. (List the costs.)
   - Path 2 (blue/gold): Join the system. In [X] days, [outcome]. (List the gains.)
   - "Which path are you on?"

3. **Cost of Inaction** (Slide 22) — BEFORE price reveal:
   - 3-4 red X-marked pain points
   - "What's it costing you to NOT have this system?"
   - Closing line about undivided attention + automation

4. **Price Reveal** (Slide 23):
   - Crossed-out total value: ~~$81,000~~
   - Actual price: $5,000 to start
   - Monthly: + $1,500/mo
   - Supporting line about your investment to learn the method

5. **Referral Bonus** (Slide 24):
   - "Bring [X] aligned people → [incentive]"
   - Define "aligned" clearly

6. **Thank You** (Slide 25):
   - Image + warm closing message

**Emotional Pillar:** FOMO — "the cost of NOT acting is higher than the cost of acting."

## THE 6 CORE BELIEFS (Slide 11 Framework)

Every prospect needs to shift 6 beliefs before buying:

| # | Belief | Old → New |
|---|--------|-----------|
| 1 | **Core** | Old method → New method |
| 2 | **Real Problem** | What they think → What actually is |
| 3 | **Time** | "I'll do it later" → "NOW is the time" |
| 4 | **Money** | "Better spent elsewhere" → "Best investment I can make" |
| 5 | **Method** | "Old ways work fine" → "This is the only proven path" |
| 6 | **Help** | "I'll figure it out alone" → "Expert help is THE path" |

**Visual treatment:** Red/blue glossy cards with pill badges (see design-system.md).

## CLOSE SEQUENCE VARIATIONS BY TEMPLATE SIZE

| Template | Close Slides | Sequence |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **VSL (15)** | 2 slides | Price Reveal → CTA |
| **Short (25)** | 6 slides | If All I Did Was → Two Paths → Cost of Inaction → Price → Referral → Thank You |
| **Standard (60)** | 10 slides | Story interludes between stack + full close sequence + FAQ + guarantee |
| **Full (125)** | 15+ slides | Complete Perfect Webinar close (per the full Perfect Webinar template) |
